📖 How to Find UTR/Transaction ID

📱 Google Pay (GPay)

  1. Open Google Pay app
  2. Tap on your profile picture → "Activity"
  3. Select the payment you just made
  4. Scroll down to find "UPI transaction ID"
  5. It's a 12-digit number (e.g., 402912345678)

📱 PhonePe

  1. Open PhonePe app
  2. Go to "History" tab
  3. Tap on the transaction
  4. Look for "Transaction ID" or "UTR"
  5. Copy the 12-digit number

📱 Paytm

  1. Open Paytm app
  2. Go to "Passbook"
  3. Select the payment transaction
  4. Find "Transaction ID" or "Order ID"
  5. Note down the 12-digit number

📱 Other UPI Apps

  1. Open your UPI app
  2. Go to transaction history
  3. Select the completed payment
  4. Look for "UTR", "Reference Number", or "Transaction ID"

💡 Important: The UTR/Transaction ID is generated only after successful payment. Make sure your payment is completed before entering the UTR number.
